Profile

Toxicology researcher studying how e-cigarette use affects male reproductive health. My PhD at Universiti Malaya compares cigarette smokers, e-cigarette users and dual users in Malaysia, linking exposure to testosterone-related microRNAs, mitochondrial function and blood cell changes. I have published 13 peer-reviewed papers as sole or first author and review manuscripts for seven international journals. Open to roles in research, medical affairs, regulatory science and science communication.
