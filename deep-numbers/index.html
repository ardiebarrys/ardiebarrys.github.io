The idea

Don't find the right answer. Find the answer everyone else will give.

Deep Numbers is a five-question daily game about predicting people. Sometimes you may know the factual answer. That does not mean it will score highest. The winning move is to predict what the crowd is most likely to enter.

01 / GUESS

One guess per question

Everyone sees the same five questions. You lock one numerical answer for each.

02 / THINK

Think like everyone else

Round numbers, familiar dates, common habits and human bias become part of the strategy.

03 / REVEAL

No feedback until the end

Your answers stay hidden while you play. The crowd and your final trajectory are revealed together.
