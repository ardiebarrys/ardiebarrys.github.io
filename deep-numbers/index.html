Trust the estimate.

Deep Numbers is designed around one simple tension: you are not trying to know the answer, you are trying to predict the answer the crowd is likely to produce.

01 · Estimate Enter one percentage from 0 to 100. Do not overthink it.
02 · Lock Your benchmark stays hidden until all five rounds are complete.
03 · Launch Your overall similarity becomes your rocket's trajectory score.

How the scoring works.

Each question has a hidden benchmark and tolerance. A close estimate scores higher than a distant one. The final mission score is the average of the five round scores.

100 Near-perfect crowd match.
50 Mixed trajectory. You caught part of the pattern.
0 Wildly off-course. The crowd went another way.
