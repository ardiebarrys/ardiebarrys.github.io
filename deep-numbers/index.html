The idea

Turn numerical rarity into a journey.

Deep Numbers is a daily game built around a simple experiment: everyone receives the same numerical space, everyone makes one irreversible pick, and the crowd determines how far each number travels.

01 / PICK

Choose one number

The same range opens for everyone. Your choice is your launch.

02 / CROWD

Rarity becomes distance

Numbers surrounded by fewer neighboring choices travel deeper into space.

03 / LOCK

The mission closes

When the mission ends, the final trajectory and rankings are locked.
