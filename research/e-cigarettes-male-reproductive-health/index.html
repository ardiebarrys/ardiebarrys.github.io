Research/ Doctoral research
Doctoral research

Does vaping change how the male body makes testosterone?

Millions of people vape. Very little is known about what the aerosol does to sexual and hormonal function. My thesis takes that question to people rather than to a model: it compares cigarette smokers, e-cigarette users and dual users, and asks whether their exposure tracks with the molecular systems that govern testosterone production.

Data collection and analysis in progress

Three groups, one comparison

Most vaping research compares users against non-users. The more informative comparison is between the exposures themselves, because dual users carry both.

Cigarette smokersThe exposure we already have decades of evidence for.
E-cigarette usersA different aerosol: metals, carbonyls, particles, no combustion.
Dual usersThe most common real-world pattern, and the least studied.

What I measure

Each endpoint is chosen because a mechanism predicts it, not because it is convenient to collect.

01

Testosterone signaling

Hormonal profile, and whether exposure tracks with the steroidogenic pathway rather than with smoking status alone.

02

Testosterone-related microRNAs

Circulating microRNAs that regulate steroidogenic transcripts, as a readout upstream of the hormone itself.

03

Mitochondrial function

Steroidogenesis begins in the mitochondrion. If energy handling shifts, hormone synthesis has a route to follow it.

04

Blood cell and redox changes

Haematological and oxidative markers, to place any hormonal signal in its systemic context.

05

Sexual desire and dependence

The human endpoint. Comparative data across smokers, vapers and dual users, presented at the ISAM regional meeting.

06

Exposure characterization

What is actually inhaled, so that device and pattern are variables rather than assumptions.

The groundwork, already published

Before measuring anything in people, I mapped what each endpoint could plausibly show, and where the existing evidence runs out. These reviews are the mechanistic basis for the design above.
