Highlighted Study

E-cigarettes and erectile dysfunction

E-cigarettes and erectile dysfunction: biological mechanisms and research challenges
June 2026, International Journal of Impotence Research
DOI: 10.1038/s41443-026-01300-0

This review explains how e-cigarette use may affect men's sexual health. E-cigarette aerosols can disturb blood vessel function, hormones, inflammation and other systems involved in erections. The evidence is still early, so stronger long-term studies are needed before we can say whether e-cigarette use directly causes erectile dysfunction.
