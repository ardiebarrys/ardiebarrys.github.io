Independent framework Cellular Signaling as Dynamic Regulatory Circuits Signaling pathways read as dynamic circuits, not on/off switches. Timing, feedback and resolution shape the outcome. Progress 45%
From pathway diagrams to control systems
  1. 01 · Problem

    Static diagrams

    Pathway maps hide signal duration and feedback.

  2. 02 · Input

    Signal arrives

    Magnitude, duration and context carry information.

  3. 03 · Control

    System responds

    Feedback, thresholds and kinetics shape the response.

  4. 04 · Resolution

    Signal ends

    Termination is part of the mechanism.

  5. 05 · Failure

    Dynamics break

    Persistent activation can turn adaptation into pathology.

Core question. Can pathways be understood better by modeling their dynamics, feedback and resolution, not only their molecular parts?
