Start with the endoplasmic reticulum

The endoplasmic reticulum (ER) helps newly made proteins fold into working shapes. When unfolded or misfolded proteins accumulate, its stress sensors activate the unfolded protein response (UPR).

  1. Protein-folding demand exceeds the ER's available capacity.
  2. Three sensors, IRE1α, PERK and ATF6, coordinate distinct responses.
  3. Folding support and protein clearance can help restore balance.

Outcomes depend on the cell type, stress intensity and recovery capacity. Persistent stress can disrupt cell function; the result is not the same in every cell.
